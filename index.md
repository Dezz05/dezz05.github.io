## ✨ What is leanstudio?

leanstudio is a **desktop application** that helps you write and check mathematical proofs using a powerful tool called **Lean 4**. Think of Lean 4 as a super-smart calculator for logic — it makes sure every step of your reasoning is correct.

But leanstudio isn't just for math geniuses. It's built for **everyone** who wants to learn, explore, or create formal proofs. Whether you're a student, a researcher, or just curious about how computers can verify human reasoning, leanstudio gives you a friendly, visual workspace to work in.

The best part? **Every proof you write is re-checked by Tenet** — an independent proof-checking engine. This means you get double assurance that your work is correct, even if you make a mistake.

## 📖 Understanding Proofs (A Quick Tour)

If you're new to Lean 4, here's a tiny taste of what it feels like:

Imagine you want to prove that `1 + 1 = 2`. In leanstudio, you type something like:

```lean
example : 1 + 1 = 2 := by
  norm_num
```

Then, **Tenet** (the independent checker) looks at your proof and confirms it's correct. If you made a mistake, leanstudio highlights the error and gives you hints to fix it.

It's like having a patient, super-smart tutor watching over your shoulder — but instead of judging you, it just helps you get it right.
